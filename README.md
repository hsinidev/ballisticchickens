Game Overview


Ballisticchickens is a fast-paced, action-packed browser game that challenges players to navigate their vehicle through obstacles while collecting eggs and avoiding enemies.



How to Play


To play Ballisticchickens, follow these steps:





Control Your Vehicle:
 Use the arrow keys or mouse to control your ballistic chicken vehicle.



Catch Enemies and Collect Eggs:
 Avoid enemies by shooting them with your blaster, while collecting eggs on the side of the screen.



Upgrade Your Vehicle:
 Earn coins and upgrade your vehicle's speed, health, and defense to improve its chances of survival.





Tips and Tricks for Success


To become a Ballisticchicken master, try these tips and tricks:





Use Your Time Wisely:
 Plan your route and optimize your path to minimize time spent in the most danger zones.



Stay Mobile:
 Keep moving to avoid taking damage from enemies and obstacles.



Upgrade Thoroughly:
 Prioritize upgrades that improve your vehicle's performance, as they can make a significant difference in survival.
